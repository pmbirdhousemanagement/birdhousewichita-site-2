About Birdhouse

Every home is a nest. We treat it that way.

Birdhouse is a locally owned property management company in Wichita, built for individual landlords who want trust and clarity more than the lowest fee.

Our story

We started Birdhouse Property Management because we saw the problem small landlords face while investing locally ourselves. Each of us built a small residential portfolio in Wichita and discovered that nobody managed properties as well as we did.

Once our own operations were running well, we opened this boutique management offering to serve other owners and manage their properties the way we manage our own. Every property runs on the same best practices, with care tailored to each owner and what their portfolio demands.
