## Blog post share block

Every post gets the share row (LinkedIn, Facebook, X, Email, Copy link) twice: under the byline and above "Back to blog" at the end. Copy it from `wichita-investing.html` (the `<div class="share">` block, the `.share`/`.sh` CSS, and the copy-link `<script>` before `</body>`), then replace the post URL and title in the share links.
